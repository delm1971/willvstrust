Build a complete budget

An advertised document price is a starting point. Before buying, make a short list of what you expect to receive and what you will still need to arrange. This helps you compare an inexpensive document with a broader package without treating unlike services as equivalent. Ask which tasks are included, which require additional help, and which costs may recur.

EZ Will & Trust pricing snapshot

As checked on October 13, 2026, EZ Will & Trust lists a will package at $149 and trust packages at $550–$650. Its homepage states that editing and storage are included for one year, followed by $19.99 per year. Prices and terms may change; confirm the package and renewal details before checkout. Costs to ask about before checkout

  • Execution: Are printing, witnessing, or notarization arrangements included?
  • Property transfers: Will anyone prepare or record deeds if your plan requires them?
  • Updates: What does later editing cost, and how do you complete revised documents?
  • Individual advice: Is an attorney consultation included or purchased separately?
  • Access: What happens to downloads and stored files if you stop renewing?

A simple comparison worksheet

For each option, record four numbers: the initial price, expected outside expenses, the cost of any professional advice, and the recurring charge. Then record four deliverables: documents, instructions, support, and follow-through assistance. Leave unknown items marked “ask provider” instead of guessing. A slightly higher starting price can be easier to evaluate when the scope is clear.

Avoid comparing a template with full representation

A guided document platform and an attorney engagement may have different responsibilities. Ask who evaluates your circumstances, who answers legal questions, and who helps resolve conflicts in your instructions. Compare the service you need rather than assuming that every estate planning purchase offers the same level of advice.

Choose a manageable next action

Read the package description, save the current terms, and list anything you cannot confirm. If the questions concern legal consequences rather than site navigation or billing, take them to a licensed attorney. If you are comfortable with the scope, visit the provider to confirm today’s offer before making a purchase.

Common questions

Are the listed prices guaranteed?

No. This is a dated snapshot. The provider’s current checkout and terms control.

Is the cheapest option always the best value?

No. Compare scope, outside expenses, support, and the work you will need to complete yourself.
