What the provider says it offers

EZ Will & Trust describes a guided DIY process using attorney-reviewed templates, with will and revocable living trust packages. Users review, download, and complete their documents. The provider says it does not directly offer personalized legal advice or attorney representation. Attorney-reviewed templates should not be confused with an attorney reviewing your individual circumstances.

Published costs at a glance

The advertised will package is $149; trust packages are $550–$650. One year of editing and storage is listed, with $19.99 annually thereafter. Confirm current terms and the applicable package before checkout. The cost comparison guide explains how to evaluate expenses outside the advertised purchase price.

What to verify before buying

  • State fit: Is the service available and appropriate for your jurisdiction?
  • Scope: Which documents does your selected package include?
  • Support: Who answers technical questions and who answers legal ones?
  • Completion: Which signing and transfer tasks remain your responsibility?
  • Terms: How do renewal, cancellation, storage, and later revisions work?

How to evaluate the DIY approach

If you prefer a self-directed process, compare the instructions and support with the work you are prepared to do. If you want a professional to evaluate your circumstances or explain legal tradeoffs, determine how to obtain that help before buying. The relevant question is whether the service’s actual scope matches your needs.
